Privacy Policy

Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

OmniCalc is designed to be a local, private way to calculate and plan your everyday finances and tools. This policy explains what OmniCalc does and does not collect.

What we do not collect

OmniCalc does not require an account. We do not sell user data, and we do not use advertising networks inside the app. We do not build a profile of your behavior for marketing or analytics.

What stays on your device

OmniCalc stores calculation history, favorites, saved tools, and preferences locally on your device so the app can function. This includes:

  • Calculation history and pinned favorite tools
  • Saved road trip plans, loan schedules, and Kameti records
  • Selected regional currency and haptic intensity

Camera and text extraction

If you use OmniCalc to scan a receipt or printed numbers, that processing happens 100% on-device using Apple's Vision framework. The extracted text is used only for calculation within the app. No photos or video frames are uploaded or transmitted to any external server.

Permissions

OmniCalc may ask for access to device features such as Camera (for receipt scanning) or Location (for GPS land boundary measurement in Zameen Naap). These permissions are used exclusively to deliver the feature you choose to use.

Retention

Data remains in the app until you delete it or remove the app. If you clear history, it is removed permanently from local storage.
