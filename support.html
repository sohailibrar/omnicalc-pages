Common questions

Does OmniCalc work without internet?
Yes. All 79+ mathematical tools, camera OCR receipt scanning, and land boundary tools run 100% offline on your device.
How does camera receipt scanning work?
Tap the camera icon on the Home tab. OmniCalc uses on-device Apple Vision text recognition to extract values locally. No images are uploaded to any server.
How do I change currency or units?
Open Settings to switch between Pakistani Rupee (PKR), USD, EUR, and other regional currencies, or adjust haptic intensity.
Where are my calculations stored?
Everything is stored exclusively on your device. No user accounts, registration, or external cloud servers are used.

Before you contact us, make sure you are on the latest version of OmniCalc from the App Store. If the issue is tied to a specific calculation or scanner reading, including a short description will help us troubleshoot faster.
